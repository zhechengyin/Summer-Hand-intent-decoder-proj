30-SECOND CATCH-UP
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Task: decode monkey 2D finger VELOCITY from 8 spike-detection channels, on an
STM32, in REAL TIME -> the decoder must be STRICTLY CAUSAL (no future frames).
Metric = R^2. Only the indy/loco monkey data.

Model of record: models/tcn_gru_8ch/ — strictly-causal wide TCN+GRU (bidir=False,
F64/H64/L1), 8 firing channels [26,51,53,66,71,73,75,94], 24 sessions, 40 ms bins,
+ multiscale input (raw + EWMA = 16 features). ~74 kB int8 (lossless), 75,714 params.
Honest TEST R^2 ~= 0.63 (eval-valid). Multiscale (LOG-068) is the only model-side
lever that beat the 0.606 single-scale causal ceiling; it helped on eval AND test.

eval-valid alpha=0.1 -> 0.630; alpha is within noise). And test1 is now BURNED

------------------------------------------------------------------------
DEAD ENDS — DO NOT RE-RUN (already ruled out; see DAILY_LOG)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Architecture: LSTM (ties GRU), Transformer/plain-CNN+GRU/TCN-only/GRU-only (worse).
  TCN+GRU wins.                                                   (LOG-060/066)
- Capacity: plateaus ~220 KB, overfits at 400 KB.                 (LOG-061)
- More data: 24 nearby sessions is the limit; distant sessions hurt. (LOG-065)
- Channel selection: firing-8 on base-6 best; learned/low-freq/fft/re-select lose/tie. (LOG-063)
- Binning: 40 ms boxcar best; finer/coarser worse; overlapping windows no help. (LOG-061)
- Output smoothing (Bessel/EMA): redundant/hurts (target already 3 Hz LP). (LOG-050/064)
- Causal arch tweaks (more RF/depth/width): no gain.             (LOG-064)
- Cheap training tricks (correlation loss, stronger aug, more reg): wash. (LOG-051)
- Auxiliary heads: multi-task (velocity+position) and LFADS-lite (rate recon) both
  tie/lose on EVAL — a second head adds no generalization signal. (LOG-072)
- Bidirectional / bounded-lookahead: NOT deployable (40 ms/bin => latency too high). (LOG-062)
